Frequently Asked Questions

How do I import EPUB books?
EPUB import is a premium feature. Upgrade to premium in the app settings, then use the EPUB tab in the import screen to select books from your device.
How do I increase reading speed above 400 WPM?
Free users are limited to 400 WPM. Upgrade to premium to unlock speeds up to 1000 WPM. You can upgrade from the Settings screen.
How do I restore my premium purchase?
Go to Settings → Premium section → tap "Restore Purchases". Your premium features will be restored automatically if you purchased on the same Apple ID.
What is RSVP reading?
RSVP (Rapid Serial Visual Presentation) is a speed reading technique that displays words one at a time in a fixed position, eliminating eye movement and allowing you to read faster.
What are blink breaks?
Blink breaks are periodic pauses that encourage you to blink, reducing eye strain during extended reading sessions. Research shows blink rate decreases significantly during reading.
How do I import articles from URLs?
URL import is a premium feature. Upgrade to premium, then use the URL tab in the import screen to paste any article URL.
Can I get a refund?
Refunds are handled by Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, find the purchase, and request a refund.
